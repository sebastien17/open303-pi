no CC needed: accent fires on velocity ≥ 100, and slide on legato (overlapping notes) — both handled inside the engine. Pitch bend is a message of its own, not a CC (±2 semitones).

gotchas: CC25 at 0 is -60 dB sustain, i.e. the original 303's percussive envelope — setting the whole extended block to 0 makes the synth nearly silent. CC73 has no effect on accented notes (their decay is fixed at 200 ms). CC23 stays subtle by design: it runs at the x4 oversampled rate, so it only ever filters a fraction of a percent.
